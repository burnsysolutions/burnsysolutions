### Scott Burns

AI automation and recruitment systems. Former founder and MD of a UK and UAE recruitment group: teams of up to 30 recruiters, and more than 500 roles filled in its last full year.

I build the systems recruitment and service businesses run on, hands-on, with AI doing the heavy lifting and people approving what matters.

#### What I've built

- **Avolon Group** (construction recruitment): an operations platform alongside Bullhorn. It puts registrations into Bullhorn with their documents, builds ranked shortlists for new vacancies, reads timesheets with AI, and runs payroll and invoicing. Its automated availability checks contacted 1,478 candidates and updated 115 records in seven weeks with no consultant time.
- **Ignite Search and Selection**: a JobAdder-style recruitment CRM holding 63,000+ candidates and nearly 130,000 CV files, recovered from a JobAdder backup.
- **Construction site intelligence**: planning data, HM Land Registry, Companies House and contract notices joined together to find sites before they start, with a tailored demo for each prospect.
- **AI CV pipeline**: generate, audit and fix on Claude, plus a CV formatting product for agencies with Stripe subscriptions.
